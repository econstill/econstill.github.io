Theory Watch

Theoretical economics on SSRN

Theory Watch is a public reading list for researchers in economic theory. I built it to make new working papers on SSRN easier to discover and to complement arXiv-based theory feeds.

It covers game theory, mechanism and market design, information and contracts, decision and social choice, equilibrium theory, and related theoretical work.

Coverage

Papers are discovered through public SSRN DOI records deposited with Crossref. Open-source MiniLM and DeBERTa models screen titles and available abstracts for economic-theory topics and distinguish theoretical contributions from primarily empirical or unrelated work. Borderline records are withheld for review. Explicit JEL codes are used when present in deposited metadata; this is not an official SSRN subject-category feed. This index can miss relevant papers and include false matches. Inclusion is not a quality assessment.

Dates above refer to DOI registration in Crossref, not the day a paper first appeared on SSRN. Indexing can be delayed. Changes to a record do not necessarily mean a new paper version.

The archive tracks DOI registrations from $coverage_start onward.

Abstracts are retrieved on request from Crossref, attributed to their authors, and not stored in this archive. Availability depends on the source record. Please consult SSRN for the latest version.
