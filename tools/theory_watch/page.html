Coverage

Papers are discovered through public SSRN DOI records deposited with Crossref. Titles and abstracts are matched automatically against economic-theory topics. This index can miss relevant papers and include false matches. Inclusion is not a quality assessment.

Dates above refer to DOI registration in Crossref, not the day a paper first appeared on SSRN. Indexing can be delayed. Changes to a record do not necessarily mean a new paper version.

The archive tracks DOI registrations from $coverage_start onward.
