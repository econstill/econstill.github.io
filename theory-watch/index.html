Coverage

Papers are discovered through public SSRN DOI records deposited with Crossref. An open-source MiniLM model retrieves candidates by title and abstract meaning, rather than author keywords. Transparent text checks then look for formal theoretical contributions and empirical evidence. Borderline records are held aside rather than automatically included. Explicit JEL codes are used when present in deposited metadata; this is not an official SSRN subject-category feed. This index can miss relevant papers and include false matches. Inclusion is not a quality assessment.

Dates above refer to DOI registration in Crossref, not the day a paper first appeared on SSRN. Indexing can be delayed. Changes to a record do not necessarily mean a new paper version.

The archive tracks DOI registrations from 30 Sep 2026 onward.

Abstracts are retrieved on request from Crossref, attributed to their authors, and not stored in this archive. Availability depends on the source record. Please consult SSRN for the latest version.
