Theory Watch

Selected microeconomic theory on SSRN

Theory Watch is a public reading list for researchers in microeconomic theory. I built it to make new working papers on SSRN easier to discover and to complement arXiv-based theory feeds.

The focus is on contributions to game theory and networks, mechanism and market design, information and contracts, decision and social choice, general equilibrium foundations, and theoretical industrial organization. Using a game, an equilibrium, or an optimization problem is not enough on its own.

Coverage

Discovery runs daily using public SSRN DOI records deposited with Crossref. An open-source MiniLM model and transparent text checks identify candidates, not public recommendations. Candidates do not automatically appear here or in RSS. This is not an official SSRN subject-category feed.

Selection considers the research question, its connection to microeconomic theory, and the stated analytical contribution. Brief contribution notes accompany the selections. Screening is Codex-assisted and its extent is recorded in the selection record; it is not peer review, proof verification, or a guarantee of novelty or quality. Author reputation and institutional affiliation are not admission rules.

AI-assisted content review is scheduled daily, separately from cloud-based collection. The review runs on the maintainer's Codex host and requires it to be running. Each batch prioritizes recent candidates and also works through the older backlog. Uncertain records are deferred; there is no daily publication quota.

The focus excludes primarily empirical, calibrated macro-finance, management, and engineering optimization papers without a substantive microeconomic-theory contribution. Foundational work on preferences or general equilibrium remains in scope even when it has macroeconomic or financial applications. Coverage is selective and incomplete. Not being selected is not a judgment that a paper is poor research.

Added is the date a paper was selected for this list. DOI registered refers to registration in Crossref, not the day a paper first appeared on SSRN. Indexing can be delayed. Changes to a record do not necessarily mean a new paper version.

Discovery tracks DOI registrations from 30 Sep 2026 onward. Collection dates are separate from selection reviews.

Abstracts are retrieved on request from Crossref, attributed to their authors, and not stored in this archive. Availability depends on the source record. Please consult SSRN for the latest version.
