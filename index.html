About

I am a doctoral student in Applied Economics at the School of Economics and Management, Beijing Jiaotong University. You can call me Still.

My current projects study strategic behavior with strong network externalities, using tools from game theory, fixed-point theory, and strategic elasticity.

Research Interests

  • Game theory and network games
  • Network externalities and pricing
  • Equilibrium existence, uniqueness, and stability
  • Strategic elasticity and fixed-point methods
