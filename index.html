About

I am a doctoral student in Applied Economics at the School of Economics and Management, Beijing Jiaotong University, advised by Zhigang Cao . My current research studies strategic behavior with strong network externalities, using tools from game theory, fixed-point theory, and strategic elasticity.

Education

  • 2023-present. Ph.D. in Applied Economics, School of Economics and Management, Beijing Jiaotong University.
  • 2021-2023. M.A. student in Applied Economics, School of Economics and Management, Beijing Jiaotong University; continued into the Ph.D. program after the second year.
  • 2015-2019. B.A. in Advertising, School of Journalism and Communication, Jilin University.

Research Interests

  • Game theory and network games
  • Network externalities and pricing
  • Equilibrium existence, uniqueness, and stability
  • Strategic elasticity and fixed-point methods
