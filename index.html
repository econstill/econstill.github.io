About

I am a doctoral student in Applied Economics at the School of Economics and Management, Beijing Jiaotong University, advised by Zhigang Cao . My current research focuses on game theory and network games, especially equilibrium existence, uniqueness, and stability.

Research Interests

  • Game theory and network games
  • Equilibrium existence, uniqueness, and stability
  • General equilibrium theory
