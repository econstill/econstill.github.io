About

Mathematical models for strategic behavior in networks.

I am a doctoral student in Applied Economics at the School of Economics and Management, Beijing Jiaotong University. My research focuses on how incentives, network structure, and equilibrium concepts shape economic outcomes.

Current projects study negative network externalities, strategic elasticity, strong network externalities, fixed-point methods, and the interpretation of nonexistence results in game theory and economics.

Research

Themes

01

Network Games

Pricing, externalities, and equilibrium behavior when agents' decisions interact through social or economic networks.

02

Equilibrium Theory

Existence, uniqueness, stability, and the economic meaning of equilibrium nonexistence in game-theoretic models.

03

Strategic Elasticity

Fixed-point and contraction methods for strategic interactions with inelastic best-response structures.
